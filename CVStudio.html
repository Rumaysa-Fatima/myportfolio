AI CV Studio

Balancing AI guidance with creative freedom in portfolio building

Role UX Researcher & Designer
Team 5 members
Duration 12 weeks
Tools Figma, Google forms, Indesign

Context

The Challenge

University students struggle to create portfolios that feel authentic. Existing AI tools automate too much, stripping away individuality. Templates feel generic. Students need guidance without losing creative control.

CV Studio provides contextual AI suggestions—design recommendations, accessibility checks, and content feedback—while keeping students in the driver's seat.

Research

Understanding User Needs

10 Interviews
5 Participants
2 Test Rounds
36 Questions

We conducted semi-structured interviews with students across disciplines—design, commerce, fashion—to understand how they approach portfolio creation. Each 15-25 minute session revealed pain points with existing tools and expectations for AI assistance.

Key Findings

  • Balance is critical — Design students want suggestions, not automation. AI should enhance, not replace.
  • Content matters more than design — Students struggle most with articulating their experience, not visual layout.
  • Integration saves time — Linking LinkedIn, Adobe, and Pinterest reduces repetitive work.
  • Templates build confidence — Non-designers need proven structures but still want customization.
  • Portfolios evolve — Version history is essential for ongoing updates.
"When creating my CV, the hardest part was thinking about the job descriptions for each role and how to properly articulate them."
"I want full freedom and control over the design of my resume because you never know when there are things I want to add."

Design

Core Features

Four Creation Pathways

Choose your starting point: blank canvas, curated templates, PDF upload, or AI-guided builder

Contextual AI Suggestions

Optional feedback on design and content—always presented as suggestions, never forced

AI Chatbox

Ask specific questions for customized changes with built-in conversation history

Third-Party Integration

Auto-sync with LinkedIn, Adobe, and Pinterest to reduce manual updates

Template Analytics

See which designs perform best in your industry for data-informed decisions

Version History

Save and revert to previous designs as your portfolio evolves

Testing

Iterative Refinement

We tested with 5 participants across two rounds, using task-based scenarios and think-aloud protocol. Each round revealed new insights that shaped the final design.

What We Fixed

1

Clarified Creation Options

Added icons and simplified wording after users confused the four pathways

2

Improved Toolbar Clarity

Added hover text when users misidentified the save icon as "print"

3

Enhanced Navigation

Made the return-to-dashboard path more explicit with clear labeling

4

Relocated App Integration

Moved to dedicated tab after users struggled to locate the feature

"As a non-designer I would not know that app panel refers to LinkedIn and other social medias. I would assume to update information under my profile."

Reflection

What I Learned

AI Should Augment, Not Replace

Users want control over their creative output. Suggestions work; automation alienates.

Testing Reveals the Unexpected

Two rounds uncovered issues we never anticipated—user feedback is irreplaceable.

One Size Doesn't Fit All

Multiple pathways serve diverse needs without overwhelming anyone.

Think-Aloud Captures More

Observing gestures and hearing thoughts revealed pain points surveys would miss.

Next Steps

  • Add interactive tutorial for first-time users
  • Build community tab for peer feedback
  • Implement autosave to reduce cognitive load
  • Test with working professionals for long-term use cases
