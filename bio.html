Hi, I am Rumaysa!

I'm a UI/UX design student with a background in visual design and a love for systems that feel effortless and human.
To me, design is care, whether it's an interface, a journey, or a button, I aim to make it clear, warm, and purposeful. I'm drawn to accessibility, quiet storytelling, and experiences that guide rather than overwhelm.
I'm still exploring my niche, but my direction is intentional: to build with empathy, design with meaning, and quietly improve lives through clarity, function, and beauty.
