Oud Collection Redesign

Transforming a fragrance website into a luxury digital experience

View on Behance →

Context

The Challenge

A luxury fragrance brand's website failed to match its premium products. Inconsistent design, cluttered layouts, and poor navigation undermined trust and usability. The goal: create a digital experience worthy of the brand's exclusivity.

Problems

What Wasn't Working

Inconsistent Visual Identity

Typography and colors were disjointed, failing to build user trust or convey luxury

Poor Navigation

Oversized menus disrupted flow and overwhelmed users trying to browse products

Cluttered Layouts

Information was scattered with weak hierarchy, making content difficult to process

Misaligned Brand

Despite claiming luxury status, the site failed to visually convey exclusivity

The target audience—middle to high-income fragrance enthusiasts—expects refined digital experiences that match product quality.

Solution

The Transformation

100% Brand Consistency
4 UX Problems Solved

Reflection

What I Learned

What Worked Well

  • Dark luxury palette immediately elevated brand perception and user trust
  • Simplified navigation dramatically improved user flow and reduced cognitive load
  • Strategic content organization made product discovery intuitive and efficient
  • Professional checkout process built user confidence through clear, secure design

Future Improvements

  • Homepage product recommendations could benefit from enhanced typography and better visual hierarchy
  • Search results enhancement with price range filters would improve product discovery
  • Overall spacing consistency could be refined further for more polished professional feel
