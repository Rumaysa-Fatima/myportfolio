Context

Project Overview

This project focused on redesigning an existing luxury fragrance brand's collection website as part of a semester-long university assignment. The primary objective was to identify and resolve key usability, aesthetic, and structural issues that were affecting user experience and brand perception. The goal is to transform the website into a luxurious, trustworthy, and intuitive digital platform that reflects the exclusivity and aesthetic refinement of the products. The target users are fragrance enthusiasts seeking premium experiences—both in product and presentation.
