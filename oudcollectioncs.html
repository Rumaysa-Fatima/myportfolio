Context

Project Overview

This project focused on redesigning an existing luxury fragrance brand's collection website as part of a semester-long university assignment. The primary objective was to identify and resolve key usability, aesthetic, and structural issues that were affecting user experience and brand perception. The goal is to transform the website into a luxurious, trustworthy, and intuitive digital platform that reflects the exclusivity and aesthetic refinement of the products. The target users are fragrance enthusiasts seeking premium experiences—both in product and presentation.

Results

Design Impact

The redesign successfully transforms the brand's digital presence into a cohesive, luxury experience that matches their premium product quality.

Key Improvements Delivered

🎯

Streamlined Navigation

Simplified oversized navigation into clean, intuitive system with strategic logo placement and improved mobile responsiveness.

📱

Enhanced Hierarchy

Product names, prices, and CTAs now clearly emphasized with thoughtful typography and proper spacing that guides user attention.

🛍️

Optimized Shopping

Product details reorganized for easy scanning, with clean cart design and professional payment process that builds user confidence.

🔍

Improved Search

Search interface redesigned to stand out when active while maintaining luxury aesthetic and better site integration.

Reflection

What I Learned

Every project teaches valuable lessons. Here's what worked well and what I'd improve in future iterations.

What Worked Well

  • Dark luxury palette immediately elevated brand perception and user trust
  • Simplified navigation dramatically improved user flow and reduced cognitive load
  • Strategic content organization made product discovery intuitive and efficient
  • Professional checkout process built user confidence through clear, secure design

Future Improvements

  • Homepage product recommendations could benefit from enhanced typography and better visual hierarchy
  • Fragrance notes section needs horizontal layout optimization to eliminate awkward spacing
  • Search results enhancement with price range filters would improve product discovery
  • Overall spacing consistency could be refined further for more polished professional feel
